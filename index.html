DRIFT

Data Selection for LLM Instruction Tuning via On-Policy Attribution

Tsinghua University

Paper Code Rollouts BibTeX

Data selection is usually studied for efficiency. DRIFT asks whether it can also raise the ceiling and produce a better model than training on all of the data.

DRIFT starts from a model that has finished SFT on its full corpus. It samples the model's own responses to validation queries, signs them by correctness, and ranks the original corpus by its influence on those responses. Training then continues on the top 10%. Influence functions are a local approximation, and the model's own responses make much closer targets than external reference answers.
